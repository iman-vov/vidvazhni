# Image generation record

Tool: built-in ImageGen. Model preference: Terra, medium. Outputs were reviewed for flat geometry and then production PNGs were drawn as deterministic clean UI surfaces so runtime areas and alpha remain exact.

## Pass 1 — composition sketch

`Use case: ui-mockup. Black-and-white clean line sketch only of a 4:3 settings overlay over subdued existing gameplay. Large centered panel, three broad blank tabs, General controls with three segmented rows and six hero-placeholder rows; Rules has six independent rows. No text, numbers, icons, logos, heroes, or new background. Targets at least 44 px.`

## Pass 2 — colour lock

`Color the approved settings composition with flat matte slate-blue panels #2C3A55, thin solid gold lines #D8A93A, even dark outlines #2B2A33, one darker shadow tone only. Keep all geometry unchanged. No text, numbers, icons, hero portraits, bevels, gradients or glow.`

## Required negative block — included on every generation

`Do NOT use: glowing lights, bloom, lens flare, god rays, volumetric light, sparkles, magical glow, glowing windows, neon, sunset or golden-hour sky, dramatic sky, clouds or fog framing the edges, vignette, burnt or torn paper edges, 3D render look, glossy or plastic surfaces, airbrush gradients, hyper-detailed texture everywhere, HDR, teal-and-orange grading, high saturation, purple evil castle cliché, depth-of-field blur, tiny repeated details, sparkle highlights, glints, specular highlights, glowing particles or fireflies, rim light, painterly brush noise, text, labels, watermarks, signatures. Leave generous empty paper space; not every area needs detail. Also do NOT use bevels, gradients, glow, decorative ornaments, icons, letters, digits, words, characters, hero portraits.`
