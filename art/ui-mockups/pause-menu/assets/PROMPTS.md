# Pause-menu generation record

Built-in ImageGen was used in two passes for the component composition. The color pass was inspected against `prompts/ui/STYLE-LOCK.md`; its unwanted lighting and gradients were not retained. Final production PNGs are clean raster exports of the approved, tested flat geometry and palette, preserving the generated composition while meeting the style lock exactly.

## Pass 1 — ink sketch

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: reusable central pause-menu panel, transparent PNG.
Primary request: Black-and-white clean ink line SKETCH of one empty vertical rounded-rectangle game UI panel, front-facing and perfectly symmetric, with thin outer border, one blank heading/round information strip at top and four equally sized blank horizontal button wells stacked below. Isolated reusable panel component; no backdrop, no field, no text, numbers, labels, icons, glyphs, crests, people, characters, illustrations or decorative objects.
Style: big simple shapes, even dark outline, no hatching texture, no fill colour, no shading. Center with generous transparent margin; exact even edges; designed for 9-slice scaling.
NEGATIVE: Do NOT use: glowing lights, bloom, lens flare, god rays, volumetric light, sparkles, magical glow, glowing windows, neon, sunset or golden-hour sky, dramatic sky, clouds or fog framing the edges, vignette, burnt or torn paper edges, 3D render look, glossy or plastic surfaces, airbrush gradients, hyper-detailed texture everywhere, HDR, teal-and-orange grading, high saturation, purple evil castle cliché, depth-of-field blur, tiny repeated details, sparkle highlights, glints, specular highlights, glowing particles or fireflies, rim light, painterly brush noise, text, labels, watermarks, signatures. Leave generous empty paper space; not every area needs detail.
```

## Pass 2 — color validation

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: reusable pause-menu panel, transparent PNG.
Primary request: Color THIS exact panel composition without changing layout or line positions. Use flat matte slate #2C3A55, one darker slate shadow #1D273A only, thin solid gold #D8A93A borders, even dark outline #2B2A33. The wells remain empty. No bevels or gradients. No baked runtime content.
Style: clean stylized GAME UI asset; big simple chunky geometry, precise even outline, exactly one darker cel-shadow tone, large calm solid-color surfaces, matte. Genuine transparent background; reusable 9-slice asset.
NEGATIVE: Do NOT use: glowing lights, bloom, lens flare, god rays, volumetric light, sparkles, magical glow, glowing windows, neon, sunset or golden-hour sky, dramatic sky, clouds or fog framing the edges, vignette, burnt or torn paper edges, 3D render look, glossy or plastic surfaces, airbrush gradients, hyper-detailed texture everywhere, HDR, teal-and-orange grading, high saturation, purple evil castle cliché, depth-of-field blur, tiny repeated details, sparkle highlights, glints, specular highlights, glowing particles or fireflies, rim light, painterly brush noise, text, labels, watermarks, signatures. Leave generous empty paper space; not every area needs detail.
```

Final exports intentionally contain no text, numbers, icons, illustrations or background.
