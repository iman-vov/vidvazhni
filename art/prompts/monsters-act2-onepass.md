# Монстри Акту II: один промпт, одразу в кольорі

Ескізів немає: ChatGPT одразу малює в кольорі. Цей текст цілком віддаєш оркестратору ChatGPT, а він роздає трьох монстрів своїм задачам. Детальні описи двопрохідного варіанта лежать у [monsters-act2.md](monsters-act2.md).

```text
Make 3 separate concept images of creatures for our family board game "Braves". Each image becomes a 3D model through Meshy image-to-3D, so the rules below are strict. One creature per image, each as a separate task. Paint directly in color, no sketch step.

STYLE FOR ALL THREE
- Stylized fantasy game creature concept, painted tabletop-miniature look, hand-painted matte textures, like a premium board-game miniature painted by hand. Match the style of the attached reference concepts from our game.
- Big simple chunky shapes, clear silhouette, readable as a small thumbnail. Flat matte colors with exactly ONE darker shade tone per color, no gradients. Clean dark outline of even weight (about 2 px at 1000 px width).
- Detail budget: nothing smaller than 1/40 of the image width; large calm areas of one flat color.
- Lighting: flat, even, matte light from the front-left. No shine, no rim light, no cast shadow, no contact shadow.
- Square 1:1. Three-quarter FRONT view. Full body visible, centered, at least 10% empty margin on every side; nothing touches or crosses the image edges. One single clear silhouette, no scene, no props unless listed.
- Background: plain flat light grey #D9D9D9 filling the whole image. No floor, no shadow, no frame.
- Faces are friendly-funny, never scary: no teeth, no fangs, no red eyes, no blood.
- Outline color #2B2A33.

NEGATIVE FOR ALL THREE — do NOT use: glowing eyes, any glow, bloom, lens flare, god rays, volumetric light, sparkles, magical particles, embers, sparks, dust or pollen clouds, fireflies, light trails, glints, specular highlights, metallic shine, iridescence, glossy or plastic surfaces, 3D render look, HDR, teal-and-orange grading, dramatic, moonlit, sunset or golden-hour light, any background scene (sky, mountains, rocks, forge, cave, grass, ground), cast shadow, clouds or fog framing the edges, vignette, depth-of-field blur, high saturation, hyper-detailed fur strands, cracks and rubble everywhere, runes, wing veins, tiny repeated details, painterly brush noise, cropped parts, a second creature, text, labels, watermarks, signatures.

TASK 1 — Night Moth (file: night-moth.png). Attach reference: shadow-wraith concept.
A large fluffy night moth the size of a cat, cute like a plush toy. Round fuzzy body of 3 soft segments, a round head about one third of the body length with two big round eyes, a fuzzy collar, two short feathery antennae shaped like simple leaves, four broad rounded wings (front pair larger), each wing with ONE simple round eye-spot; six short legs tucked under, barely visible. Hovering calmly, wings spread wide, symmetric and flat, body level; camera about 20 degrees above, body turned about 30 degrees to the viewer's left; wingspan about 70% of the image width. Wings are 4 big simple flat shapes.
Palette only: wings and body #2F2A3A, shade #1F1B28, collar #4A4258, eye-spots #7A5FA0 (flat violet, NOT glowing), eyes #D9D4E6 with flat pupil #2B2A33, antennae #5A5068. Sleepy-cute face, no mandibles.

TASK 2 — Hammerfist, a boss (file: hammerfist.png). Attach reference: bog-miller concept.
An old blacksmith giant made of grey stone and copper, enchanted and sleepy, not evil. Huge barrel chest, short thick legs about one third of the height, small head about one sixth of the body height, very large forearms. Instead of the right hand: a big stone blacksmith hammer head bound with two copper bands, fused to the wrist. Left hand: a large open empty stone hand. A round forge opening in the middle of the chest with a simple 3-bar grate. A thick leather apron from chest to knees. A short square stone beard. 4–5 small dark moths sitting on the shoulders and apron. Relaxed A-pose: arms angled down about 45 degrees away from the body, hands not touching the body, feet flat and slightly apart; body turned about 20 degrees to the viewer's left; figure fills about 80% of the image height. Stone is 8–12 big flat facets.
Palette only: stone #8E8A82, shade #6B675F, copper #B8733E, shade #8A5530, hammer head #7C7870, apron #6B4A32, shade #4E3524, beard #A39E94, forge opening #C8642E with shade #8A3E1E as flat color (NOT glowing, no light on the body), eyes #B9B3C4 flat pale grey-violet, moths #2F2A3A. Tired, confused face like someone woken at night.

TASK 3 — Mountain Ram (file: mountain-ram.png). Attach reference: grey-wolf concept.
A stocky grey mountain ram, stubborn and grumpy, under a misty enchantment. Barrel body about 1.6 times as long as tall, short thick legs about one third of the height, heavy head held a little low, two huge horns curling ONCE around the ears in a thick simple spiral (each horn about as wide as the head), fleece as 6–8 big rounded clumps, short stub tail. Standing calmly on all four legs on one ground line, legs apart, mouth closed; body turned about 30 degrees to the viewer's left; fills about 70% of the image height. No harness, no bells, no rope.
Palette only: fleece #C9C4BA, shade #9A958C, snow clumps #EEF0F2, horns #8C7A5E, shade #6A5B45, face and legs #6F6A63, hooves #3E3A36, misty eyes #B9B3C4 (flat, NOT glowing), 3–4 flat fog curls in the wool #B9B3C4.

IF A RESULT DRIFTS, reply: "Too detailed and glossy, looks AI-generated. Redo THIS exact creature as clean game art: bigger simpler shapes, flat matte colors with one shade tone, nothing glowing, whole body with empty margin on all sides, plain light grey background, no shadow."

DELIVER: the three PNG files, square, at least 1024×1024. Save them as art/concepts/night-moth/front.png, art/concepts/hammerfist/front.png and art/concepts/mountain-ram/front.png, and copy each as v1.png in the same folder.
```
