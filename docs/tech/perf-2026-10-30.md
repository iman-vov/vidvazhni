# Board load, 2026-10-30: where the new detail went and what was cut

The boards got much heavier in two days of new detail (obstacles, terrain props, the Glass Town kit,
multi-hex sets): 0.67–1.34M triangles and up to 429 draw calls a frame on high. Goal: cut that with no
visible loss at the normal zoom. Targets: high ≤ ~600k triangles and ≤ ~300 calls on the heaviest
board, medium about half of that.

## How it was measured

`dev/board.html?board=sNN&quality=Q&perf=1` in headless Chrome on the PC (Iris Xe), the iPad's canvas
(1366×1024 CSS px at DPR 1.5), default camera, every room revealed, 4 heroes. Each board was measured
once the scene had settled (triangles and shader programs unchanged for 9 s, at least 20 s after load),
with `board.perf()`: draw calls and triangles of one frame, by pass (sun's shadow map, scene, post)
and by what is drawn, plus the estimated GPU memory. Triangles and calls do not depend on the device;
fps has to be measured on the iPad (`dev/bench.html`).

Raw data: `perf-2026-10-30/before.json`, `perf-2026-10-30/after.json`. Frames on high, before and
after: `perf-2026-10-30/<board>-high-{before,after}.jpg`.

## Where the cost was (before, high, calls / triangles, scene + sun's map)

- **s3**: env:marsh 96/459k, monsters 54/214k, env:env-flora 12/177k, heroes 26/128k, env:env-rocks 2/101k, env:model 8/86k, env:scatter 4/79k
- **s8**: monsters 67/240k, env:model 12/172k, output_unwrapped 68/171k, heroes 28/134k, env:scatter 4/47k, env:env-rocks 2/35k, static-batch 57/30k
- **s17**: env:model 16/246k, monsters 74/162k, heroes 14/116k, output_unwrapped 8/26k, env:scatter 2/25k, rack-long 4/22k, wind-up-key 4/18k
- **s18**: env:model 16/222k, monsters 37/135k, env:glass-window 2/134k, heroes 14/116k, env:glass-wall 2/92k, bookshelf 12/52k, output_unwrapped 12/36k
- **s20**: env:env-rocks 26/502k, heroes 28/134k, monsters 59/96k, env:model 10/94k, env:scatter 4/55k, static-batch 12/42k, env:rock 2/26k

- **Figures** were the one cost on every board: 230–370k triangles with their shadows. Meshy figures
  are 7–21k triangles each, and on high they carried 2048 maps (~64 MB of GPU memory per model).
- **Marsh reeds** (s3): ~900 triangles a clump, a few hundred clumps, drawn in full whenever one clump
  was in view (three culls an InstancedMesh as a whole).
- **Rock kit stones** (s20 cave walls, rubble, boulders): every stone ~1500 triangles whatever its size,
  so a pebble-sized chunk cost as much as a boulder. 500k with the shadow pass.
- **Prop models in the ring and on the hexes** (s8, s17, s18): instanced or batched per room, but the
  props of obstacle clusters (crates, barrels, sacks) were never batched (quantised GLB attributes),
  and the same model in several rooms was a draw per room.
- **Small shadow casters on high**: litter, cups, the floor scatter (whose batch-wide bounds read as a
  big caster, against scatter.ts's intent) were drawn again into the sun's map.

## What changed (commits "Perf 1/5" … "Perf 8")

1. **Figure LOD** (`figureView.ts`, `simplify.ts`). A figure under 260 device px tall draws a copy of
   its meshes simplified to 1 % of its size (meshoptimizer, normals and UVs weighed, seams closed):
   ~1 px of difference at the normal zoom (~110 px tall), ~20–25 % fewer triangles in the scene and
   the sun's map. Zoomed in close, the full mesh. The copy shares the attributes (only the index
   changes). The models' own low tier was tried first and dropped: up close it breaks up (dark
   patches on a bandit's arms, cracks through a boulder).
2. **Texture tiers on high.** Figures load the medium tier's textures (1024, same mesh) instead of
   2048, prop models the medium tier's 512 instead of 1024. On the board they look the same; GPU
   memory on high fell from 0.9–1.2 GB to ~0.5–0.6 GB. Medium and low were already at 1024/512 and
   512/256, so the "1024 → 512 on medium/low" step had nothing left that would not show (medium
   figures at 512 soften when zoomed in, and medium is the iPad default).
3. **Instance culling** (`instanceCull.ts`). The marsh and the instanced prop models pack the
   instances inside the view (grown by 1.5–3 m for shadows thrown into it) to the front whenever the
   camera moves. Same calls, fewer triangles. (The floor scatter is a BatchedMesh, culled per
   instance by three already.)
4. **Thinner marsh on medium and low**: the extras beyond the edge clumps 80 → 55 % on medium,
   50 → 35 % on low. Small in effect: most of the marsh are the clumps that close the edges.
5. **Shadows**: only big things cast on high (as on medium); the floor scatter never casts; reed
   blades stop casting on medium (as on high).
6. **Rock kit stones** (`stone.ts`, `simplify.ts`): a seam-safe simplified copy per kit stone at load,
   main stones within 1 % (~1500 → ~1000 triangles), chunks within 4 % (→ ~500). The Permissive mode
   halves it again but opened cracks in the boulders, so it is off. `meshoptimizer` moved from dev to
   runtime dependencies (its simplifier is ~70 KB with the wasm).
7. **One obstacle batch for the board** (`boardTiles.ts`, `mergeParts.ts`): rooms standing in place
   merge their obstacles and dividers together, a draw per material across the board; a room still
   rising keeps its own batch. Quantised model props (cluster crates, barrels, sacks) are merged as
   float copies, and their contact shadows go into the batch too. s8: 429 → 282 calls.

Tried and dropped: 8 m patches for lighter merged kits (trees): −60k triangles on s3 but +67 calls.

## Against the targets

- **Calls: met.** High 188–284 on all five boards (was 211–429).
- **Triangles on high: close on s20 (625k), not met on s8, s18, s17 (725–770k) and s3 (973k).** On
  s8, s17 and s18 what is left is spread out: figures (~180–250k with their shadows), the Glass Town
  kit and ring props, the batched obstacles; no single layer to cut without it showing. A figure LOD
  from the models' low tier took these boards to ~0.6M, but it broke up visibly up close, so it was
  replaced by the seam-safe copy (−20–25 % instead of −50 %). s3's remaining cost
  is the marsh in view (~300k in the scene) and the trees around it (~180k with their shadow). Both
  are the art's density where the camera looks; cutting them in code would show. Options: a lighter
  reed clump in the marsh kit (Blender, ~400 instead of ~900 triangles: blades with fewer segments,
  fewer cattails), and a far-band tree set. That is an art task, not done here.
- **Medium at half of high: not met.** Medium now draws almost what high draws (the cuts above apply
  to both), and the remaining difference between the two is in fill, not triangles: pixel ratio, map
  size, AO, lamps. Getting medium to ~300k would take visible cuts on medium: no sun shadows from the
  environment around the board (walls of rooms and obstacles keep theirs), fewer ring props and
  trees, the reed clumps above. Not done without the user's say.
- **GPU memory on high roughly halved** (0.9–1.2 GB → 0.5–0.6 GB), which matters as much on the iPad
  as triangles: the tab was killed for memory on high before.

## To check on the iPad

1. `dev/bench.html?boards=s3,s8,s17,s18,s20&qualities=high,medium` (as in perf-budget.md): fps and
   p5 against the 2026-10-28 run, and that no high run crashes the tab.
2. Zoom in close on a figure and on a crate pile: the full meshes come back at ~2.5× zoom, nothing
   pops visibly when crossing it.
3. Pan across the marsh on s3 and the shelves of s18: no reed clump or prop blinks in at the edge of
   the screen (instance culling).
4. Reveal a room on any board: its obstacles rise with it, then sit still (it joins the board's batch
   after the rise).
5. Boulders and rubble on s20 and s7: no holes or dark cracks, at the normal zoom and zoomed in.
